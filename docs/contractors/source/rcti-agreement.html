1

AviPrep issues your invoices

AviPrep may issue tax invoices for the supplies the Contractor makes to AviPrep under the Contractor Agreement, including content royalties (each an RCTI).

2

You don’t issue your own

The Contractor will not issue tax invoices for any supply covered by this agreement.

3

Timing and copies

AviPrep will issue each RCTI within 28 days after the end of the month it relates to, give the Contractor a copy with that month’s royalty statement, and keep the original. Each RCTI will be marked “Recipient created tax invoice” and show both parties’ names and ABNs.

4

GST registration

  • AviPrep confirms it is registered for GST and will tell the Contractor if that changes.
  • The Contractor confirms their GST status below and will tell AviPrep within 7 days if they register, cancel their registration, or their ABN is cancelled.
  • If the Contractor isn’t registered for GST, AviPrep will issue a recipient created invoice with no GST included instead.
5

Corrections

The Contractor will check each RCTI and tell AviPrep of any error within 14 days. AviPrep will then issue a corrected invoice or adjustment note.

6

Your tax stays yours

The Contractor remains responsible for reporting the income and for any GST and income tax payable on it.

7

Term

This agreement starts when both parties sign it and continues while the Contractor Agreement is in force. Either party may end it earlier by written notice. Invoicing then returns to the Contractor.
