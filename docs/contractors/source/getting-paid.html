Getting paid

Royalties are paid through Stripe. There are two things to do, once, and then payments land in your account each month without you lifting a finger.

1

Verify your identityAbout two minutes

Sign in at curators.aviprep.com.au, open Earnings and choose Verify with Stripe. You’ll photograph your driver licence or passport and take a selfie. We’re required to know who we’re paying before any money moves, and Stripe does this check for us.

2

Set up payoutsAbout three minutes

Once you’re verified, the payouts step unlocks on the same page. Stripe asks for your date of birth, address and bank account. Because you’ve already verified your ID, Stripe usually won’t ask for it again.

  • Photo ID

    Australian driver licence or passport

  • Bank account

    BSB and account number in your name

  • ABN, if you have one

    Send it to us and we’ll add it

3

Get paid

Nothing further to do. Payments appear on your bank statement as AVIPREP ROYALTIES.

We never see your bank details

Your ID and bank details go straight to Stripe. AviPrep only ever sees your bank’s name and the last four digits, so you can check it’s the right account. To change it later, open Earnings and manage it in Stripe.

How a month runs

Royalties are 25% of each subject’s net revenue, shared by everyone’s points on live content.

1st

We work out the month just gone and prepare your statement, and your RCTI if you have an ABN.

Within a few days

We email them to you. They’re also on your Earnings page any time.

By the 14th

The money is sent to your bank account through Stripe.

Until you’ve done both steps, your royalties still add up: they’re just held until we can pay them. If we don’t have your ABN or a signed Statement by a supplier, we have to withhold 47% of anything over $75 and send it to the ATO in your name.
