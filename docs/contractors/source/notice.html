Before we pay you

We need your ABN or a signed hobby form before your first royalty payment. Without one, the law says we have to hold back 47% of anything over $75 and send it to the ATO.

OPTION 1

Get an ABN

Recommended

It’s free and takes about ten minutes at abr.gov.au. Apply as a sole trader, then send us the number.

You declare your royalties in your tax return, so it’s worth putting a bit aside. You don’t need GST unless your turnover hits $75,000 a year.

You get $400 of a $400 royalty
OPTION 2

Sign the hobby form

If this is a hobby for you rather than a business, sign the ATO’s Statement by a supplier (NAT 3346). We’ll send it over.

If you’re earning regularly with us, the ATO may see it as a business, and the form won’t cover you.

You get $400 of a $400 royalty
OPTION 3

Do neither

We hold back 47% of each payment and pay it to the ATO for you.

It counts towards your tax when you lodge your return. We’ll send you a payment summary after 30 June.

You get $212 of a $400 royalty$188 to the ATO
